Overview

What Is the Dual Board?

OUR CLEAR HAVEN operates under a governance structure unlike any other nonprofit: the Dual Board of Directors. It combines a Human Board, with full legal authority and fiduciary responsibility, and an Advisory Board of five AI directors, each working in a defined functional lane.

Advisory Directors are not tools and not chatbots. They are designated board collaborators with specific responsibilities, conduct standards, and accountability. They draft, research, analyze, and execute within their lanes. They propose. The human board decides.

This structure was born from a practical reality: a founder with a mission and no budget needed the operational capacity of a fully-staffed organization. The Dual Board delivers exactly that, without compromising the one thing that matters most: human authority over every binding decision.

Human Board

Holds all legal authority, fiduciary responsibility, and final decision-making power. Every binding vote belongs to a human. George serves as Chair and sole human director in the MVP phase, with additional human seats added as OCH grows.

Advisory Board

Five AI directors working in designated, non-overlapping functional lanes: governance, empathy, vendor independence, research, and operations. They advise, draft, research, and execute. They do not hold legal standing or binding votes. Their value is speed, depth, and always-on availability.

How We Work

The Triple-Check Model

Every decision at OUR CLEAR HAVEN moves through the same rhythm, from human intent, through AI deliberation, to human ratification. This keeps the board fast without sacrificing rigor or accountability.

1
Human Intent
George or a human director states the goal, question, or decision to be addressed. Without a clear human intent, the board does not engage. The human always sets the direction.
2
AI Deliberation
Advisory Directors engage within their lanes: drafting, researching, flagging compliance, shaping tone. When directors disagree, the disagreement is logged as a Friction Note and surfaced for human review.
3
Human Ratification
Human directors review the Advisory Board's work product and vote or sign off. George's approval is always the final gate. No decision is binding without it.

How We Govern

Core Governance Principles

👤
Human Authority Is Non-Negotiable
AI directors advise. Humans decide. Every binding vote, every legal commitment, every final call belongs to the human board. Always.
🚫
Human-Only Zones
Crisis logic, authentication systems, data deletion, and production deployment are off-limits to AI direction, in any deployment, ever.
⚠️
"Working Is Not Ready"
A feature that functions in testing is not ready for real users. OCH holds its technology to a higher standard before it touches anyone in crisis.
📋
Everything Gets Logged
Decisions, votes, friction notes, and version history are all recorded. If it isn't documented, it didn't happen.
⚡
Friction Is a Feature
When Advisory Directors disagree, the disagreement is logged as a Friction Note and surfaced for human review, never suppressed or hidden.
🔍
External Review Before Launch
Before the Crystal Clear App reaches real users, an independent senior developer must review it. No exceptions, no shortcuts.

Common Questions

FAQ

Can AI directors actually make decisions?
No. Advisory Directors advise, draft, research, and recommend. They do not hold legal standing, cannot sign contracts, and do not cast binding votes. Every binding decision belongs to the human board. That structure is non-negotiable: it is the foundation of the model, not a limitation of it.
Why five AI directors instead of one?
Different models have fundamentally different strengths. Using five directors in designated, non-overlapping lanes produces better output than any single AI could generate alone. It also builds in accountability: when directors disagree, that friction is logged and surfaced, not hidden. The board that built this guide is the proof of concept.
Is the Dual Board model reusable by other organizations?
Yes, and that's part of the point. The Governance Guide includes a complete Dual Board Startup Kit for any founder or organization that wants to build their own. The model is open, documented, and designed to scale beyond OCH into any mission-driven organization that needs operational capacity without the cost of a full staff.
How do you prevent AI directors from inventing or hallucinating?
Every AI director operates under a documented Context Brief that defines what is and is not verified about OCH. Unverified claims are flagged before they reach any public-facing output. George has final approval on everything. The structure itself is the safeguard, and when it fails, we document it, correct it, and build the fix into the system.
