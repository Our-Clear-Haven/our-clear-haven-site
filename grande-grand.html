About the club

What is the Grande Grand Founders Club?

Our way of saying thank you to the earliest and most committed supporters who help bring the Crystal Clear App to life. You're not just donating, you're making history. Joining at the $100+ level secures a permanent place in the history of OUR CLEAR HAVEN and the Crystal Clear App.

Member benefits

Your benefits as a Grande Grand Founder

🏅 Permanent Recognition

Your name (or a name you choose) can be listed in the app as a Grande Grand Founder. Optional.

⚡ Early Access

Be among the first to test new features and versions of the Crystal Clear App before public release.

📩 Monthly Updates

Personal behind-the-scenes emails with real progress, honest challenges, and wins along the way.

🤝 Exclusive Community

Access to a private space where Founders can connect, share ideas, and follow the journey up close.

💜 Lasting Impact

The knowledge that you helped build something that will help thousands of people when they need it most.

🛍️ Exclusive Merchandise

Access to exclusive Founders Club merchandise, coming soon. Wear your support and show you were one of the first.

How to join

Three steps to becoming a Founder

1

Give $100 or more

Support OUR CLEAR HAVEN's GoFundMe with a donation of $100 or more.

2

Let us know

Email contact@ourclearhaven.org with your donation so we can confirm your Founder spot. Spots are limited to the first 1,000.

3

You're a Grande Grand Founder

You'll get your first monthly update and instructions for recognition and community access.

From the founder

A personal note

Every single Grande Grand Founder is helping turn a painful personal experience into a nationwide solution. Right now I work retail just to cover basic expenses, which leaves very little time to dedicate to building this. Your support changes that: it means I can build full-time and get this into the hands of the people who need it most.

This isn't just a donation. You're becoming part of the founding history of something I believe will genuinely change how people navigate the hardest moments of their lives. I don't take that lightly, and I won't let you down.

Pip, Squeak, and I are deeply grateful to have you with us on this journey.

— George ScharfetterFounder, OUR CLEAR HAVEN
