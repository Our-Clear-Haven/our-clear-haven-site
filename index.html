Why this exists

Built from a night I couldn't find my way out of.

I lost my housing while undergoing diagnosis for Hashimoto's disease — my first time experiencing homelessness, and I had no idea where to start. For 8+ months I've lived in Bend motels with my two ESA cats, Pip and Squeak, while resources stayed scattered across a dozen phone numbers and websites. Crystal Clear App is the thing I needed that night: one calm place, one voice, no wrong door.
